PixelScan Online – Hospital Document Scanning

AI-assisted

A web version of a desktop scan station used by hospital medical records staff. Paper documents are scanned, indexed against the patient record and filed into the OPD / IPD cabinets of the existing EMR scan database.

FastAPIPythonReact 19 PostgreSQLTWAIN scanner agentPyMuPDFBarcode (zxing)
  • Staff sign in with their existing EMR account and permissions
